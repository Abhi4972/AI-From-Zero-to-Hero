@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
New replicas take over five minutes to answer, so the autoscaler declares them unhealthy and starts more, and the spike is over before they help. Before changing flags, the case asks you to read the timing of each startup phase. Almost all of the time is copying the model's weights, which no serving flag shortens. The fix is to change the design around that fact.
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ Reading phase timings before changing settings shows which phase dominates.
- ✗ A faster image pull would fix the cold start.
  The weight copy took 325 of 330 seconds; the image was not the problem.
- ✓ Keeping replicas warm and weights on local NVMe avoids paying the load at every spike.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Read phase timings before changing anything.
- The largest phase is often one no flag controls.
- Design around it: warm replicas, local weights, persistent caches.
- Make health checks tolerate a known start time.
:::
@@
