@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
A long install inside a sandbox is gone after an hour and the sandbox shows "stopped". The developer suspects a crash and wants to rebuild everything. In fact the daemon stopped the sandbox on purpose, after the last session closed and a grace period passed, and said so in its log. The case trains a basic question: did the thing stop, fail, or get deleted, and what does the component that owns its lifecycle say?
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ "Stopped" is a state, not an error; it says nothing about a crash.
- ✗ No error on screen means nothing was logged.
  The daemon logged the stop at INFO level, in its own log.
- ✓ Starting long jobs detached keeps the sandbox from stopping when the terminal closes.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Distinguish stopped, failed and deleted.
- Read the log of the component that owns the lifecycle.
- Reattach before rebuilding; work may still be there.
- Run long jobs detached.
:::
@@

@@takeaways
