@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
A machine rebooted three times in a day under training load with nothing in the logs. The cause was never recorded, and the postmortem says so; the mitigation, a heat guard that pauses the job, worked. The case trains writing an honest postmortem: what is known, what is not, and a mitigation proven by uptime.
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ A postmortem can state that the cause is unknown.
- ✗ No panic in the log means the reboots were not caused by the run.
  The run was the only load; logs simply did not record a cause.
- ✓ Uptime after the guard is evidence the mitigation helps.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Separate known facts from guesses.
- Mitigate when the cause is open.
- Prove the mitigation with uptime.
- Say what would identify the cause next time.
:::
@@
