@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
An A/B test of CUDA graphs shows no difference, and the team plans to turn graphs off. The GPU was overheating and throttled to 360 MHz, its clock was not recorded, and the two runs did not share conditions. The comparison measured heat, not graphs. The case trains recording the conditions an experiment depends on, and calling a result void when they changed.
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ A thermally throttled GPU can hide or invert a performance difference.
- ✗ Running the two arms one after the other on the same afternoon controls for the hardware.
  The card's clock changed with temperature; it must be recorded and matched.
- ✓ The repaired comparison found graphs saved about 1 % per decode step on this model.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Record the conditions a benchmark depends on, such as clocks and temperature.
- Interleave arms or repeat them to spread drift.
- Declare a result void when conditions changed.
- Small effects need careful measurement.
:::
@@
