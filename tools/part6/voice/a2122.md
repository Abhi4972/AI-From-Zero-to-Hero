@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
Pods take six minutes to become ready, and an engineer proposes disabling CUDA graph capture to save startup time; a reviewer fears decode will slow down. Both guessed sizes without measuring. Capture took about 40 seconds of a 373-second start, because loading the weights took 322 seconds. The case trains measuring each phase's share before arguing about it.
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ Disabling graphs removed about 40 s of capture, and the start still took 346 s.
- ✗ Capture is the main cost of a slow start.
  The weight load dominated: 322 s of the start.
- ✓ On this model and card, decode time per token changed by 3 percent or less without graphs.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Measure each phase before arguing about one.
- Optimizing a small phase cannot fix a large total.
- Measure the cost of a change on both startup and serving.
- Address the dominant phase in the design.
:::
