@@top The situation
:::plain In plain words first
Six steps into a reinforcement-learning run, the reward dipped three times and a teammate wants to stop and halve the learning rate. Rewards on small batches are noisy; six points are not a trend. The manual lists what to read in the first ten steps and the healthy ranges for each. Read that way, the run is fine. The case trains judging early training by stated ranges, not by the last three numbers.
:::
@@

@@before Walkthrough
:::check
Which statements are right?

- ✓ Step-to-step reward on a small batch is noisy.
- ✗ A loss and advantages near zero at step 0 mean the run is dead.
  Centering makes them near zero by design.
- ✓ Comparing with the healthy runs' ranges is the right test.
:::
@@

@@end The lesson to carry
:::recap What to keep from this case
- Six noisy points are not a trend.
- Read every signal the manual lists, against healthy ranges.
- Near-zero values can be correct by construction.
- Do not change the learning rate on a hunch.
:::
