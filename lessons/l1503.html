Most figures in this course were drawn for it. Where a well-known teaching resource already had a clear picture of an idea, and its owner allows reuse, the course shows that picture with a credit line underneath. This page lists every such figure in one place, with its source and licence.

How outside figures are used

  • Only figures published under an open licence are included: Creative Commons licences that allow reuse (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0), the MIT and Apache 2.0 licences of open-source course and documentation projects, or the public domain (one figure from NIST, a United States government agency).
  • Each figure is shown unmodified, apart from resizing and conversion to a web image format, with its source and licence beneath it.
  • Figures under a ShareAlike licence (CC BY-SA) stay under that licence. If you pass this course on, pass the credit lines on with it.
  • Famous figures whose owners reserve all rights are not copied. Where they are worth seeing, lessons link to them in "See it drawn" and "Watch the practitioners" boxes.
